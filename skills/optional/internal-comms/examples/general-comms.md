# General Internal Communications

Default guideline for internal writing that doesn't match the other formats:
announcements, decision records, proposals, apologies, org changes.

## Format

```markdown
# <What this is> — <date>

## Who this affects and what changes
<1–3 sentences. Name the audience and the concrete change.>

## Why
<The decision or reason, briefly. Acknowledge the tradeoff if there is one.>

## What you need to do (or: nothing)
<Action items with owners and dates — or state explicitly that no action is
needed.>

## Questions
<Where to ask, and who answers.>
```

## Tone and rules

- Say the hard thing plainly, once, in the first paragraph — never bury a
  decision in paragraph six.
- No corporate hedging ("we may potentially be exploring"); commit or say
  what is uncertain and when it will be known.
- Write for the colleague on parental leave who reads it in one tab next
  Tuesday: self-contained, no required prior context.
- If it announces a change people cannot influence, say so; if feedback is
  welcome, name the channel and the deadline.

## Gather before writing

1. Who is the audience, and what is the one thing they must know or do?
2. What decision was made, by whom, and what was traded off?
3. Where do questions go, and when will the next update come?
