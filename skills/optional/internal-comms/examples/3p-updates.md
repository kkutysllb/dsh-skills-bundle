# 3P Updates (Progress / Plans / Problems)

A regular team-facing update (often weekly). Readers are peers and leads who
want signal in under two minutes.

## Format

```markdown
# <Team/Project> 3P — <date range>

## Progress
- <shipped or advanced since last update, outcome-first>
- ...

## Plans
- <what comes next, with the why in a few words>
- ...

## Problems
- <blockers, risks, or open questions; name the help you need>
- ...
```

## Tone and rules

- Lead each bullet with the outcome, not the activity ("Checkout conversion
  +4% after retry fix", not "worked on checkout").
- Plans are commitments or explicit bets; mark uncertain items as such.
- Problems are not confessions — an update with an empty Problems section for
  weeks in a row usually means risks are being hidden.
- No more than 5 bullets per section; push detail to linked docs.

## Gather before writing

1. What shipped since the last update (merge log, task board)?
2. What is actually planned next, and what changed since last week's plan?
3. What is blocked, at risk, or waiting on a decision — and from whom?
