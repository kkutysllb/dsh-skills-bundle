# Company Newsletter

Company-wide or org-wide announcements. Readers range from executives to
new hires; most will skim the first screen.

## Format

```markdown
# <Newsletter title> — <date>

## The headline
<1–2 sentences: the single most important thing, plain language>

## Also this month
- **<Topic>** — <one sentence on what changed and who it affects>
- ...

## Coming up
- <dates, events, deadlines people must act on>

## Shout-outs
- <specific, named appreciation; link the work>
```

## Tone and rules

- No jargon that only one team understands; expand acronyms on first use.
- Every item answers "what changed and who is affected".
- Deadlines and action items go in "Coming up" with the date bolded.
- Keep the whole issue scannable in 3 minutes; link out for depth.

## Gather before writing

1. What are the 1–3 things everyone genuinely needs to know?
2. Which teams shipped something worth naming, and who should be credited?
3. What dates must people act on before the next issue?
