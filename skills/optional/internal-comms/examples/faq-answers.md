# FAQ Answers

Reusable answers to frequently asked questions (support, internal help docs,
onboarding). Readers arrive with a question and low patience.

## Format

```markdown
## Q: <the question exactly as users phrase it>

<Direct answer in the first sentence — no preamble.>

<Optional: 1–3 short steps or a tiny example.>

**Related:** <link to docs, owner, or escalation path>
```

## Tone and rules

- Answer first, explain second. The first sentence must stand alone.
- Use the asker's vocabulary; if they say "login breaks", the question is
  "login breaks" — not "authentication flow behavior".
- One question per entry; split compound questions.
- If the honest answer is "it depends", state the two branches explicitly.
- Date-stamp anything likely to change (pricing, limits, supported versions).

## Gather before writing

1. What is the exact question, in the asker's own words?
2. What is the shortest correct answer, and what are the common follow-ups?
3. Who owns this answer, and where does it go when it becomes stale?
